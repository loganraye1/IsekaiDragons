# Dragon Art Assets

## Production-Approved Hatchlings

The first hatchling set is production-approved for the v0.1 art direction baseline:

- `fire_hatchling.png`
- `water_hatchling.png`
- `earth_hatchling.png`

Approval notes:

- Fire reads as an ambitious chaotic gremlin with forward-curving horns as the dominant silhouette.
- Water reads as a sleepy mystical companion with large flowing fins and soft curves.
- Earth reads as a stubborn cozy guardian with chunky crystal-plated shoulders and sturdy proportions.
- All three are approved for emotional readability, element distinction, thumbnail readability, and companion appeal.

## Asset Rules

- Transparent background
- 1:1 canvas
- Centered 3/4 portrait framing
- Readable at 128px and 200-300px phone display size
- Eyes and dominant silhouette feature should remain visible under subtle glow
