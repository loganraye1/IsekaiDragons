# Fire Hatchling — Reference Alignment Notes

Reference target from Topnotch: dark cute fire hatchling breathing fire, side-profile, chunky/appealing body, scarf, wings, horns, cracked lava scales.

## What to Match More Closely

- **Silhouette:** side-facing quadruped, big expressive head, short sturdy legs, raised wing, clear open mouth, and a compact curled tail. Do not make the tail long enough to dominate the body.
- **Proportions:** larger head and eyes, chunkier cute body, shorter legs, less abstract puppet shape.
- **Rendering:** polished painterly mobile-game concept art with clean outlines, rounded volume, warm rim light, not flat vector pieces.
- **Palette:** dark charcoal/brown scales, bright orange lava cracks, golden belly plates, cream/white scarf, orange wing membrane.
- **Head design:** angular dragon snout, expressive orange eye, black/obsidian horns with glowing cracks, cheek spikes, open jaw with teeth and tongue.
- **Living Forge motifs:** furnace chest should read as a glowing lava belly/chest plate integrated into the body, not a separate icon; tail and body cracks can glow like internal heat.
- **Attack read:** fire must come from the mouth as a shaped flame plume; no beams, no full-screen flashes.
- **Animation layers:** final rig needs head, jaw, neck, scarf, wing membrane, tail segments, tail tip, legs/claws, chest/belly glow, lava crack glow, mouth flame plume.

## What to Change From Current Rig

- Make the body/head more finished and reference-like before further animation tuning.
- Add the scarf as its own high-priority layer; it is a major identity/readability shape in the reference.
- Add wing membrane/ribs as more polished separate layers instead of rough rear wing block.
- Replace simplified geometric body with rounded, plated anatomy and lava-crack pattern.
- Replace separate symbolic chest core with integrated glowing belly/chest furnace plates.
- Add claws, cheek spikes, back spikes, and horn highlights.

## Acceptance Direction

The next Fire hatchling layer repaint should look like a polished side-profile character similar in quality and proportions to the reference, while still preserving our Living Forge identity and animation-ready separated layers.
