# First iPhone Playtest Checklist

Owner: Caldrin, with Aurelith interpreting player experience notes.

## Goal

Validate that the first 15 minutes of Isekai Dragons in Expo Go are stable, readable, understandable, and promising enough to justify the next polish pass.

## Before starting

- Start the app with Expo Go.
- Use a fresh save or reset progress.
- Keep balance version `0.1.0` unchanged.
- Turn sound/haptics/reduced motion settings on/off only if testing those specifically.

## Watch for

### Technical

- App launches reliably in Expo Go.
- No crashes or red screens.
- Taps register cleanly.
- Text is readable on the phone.
- Animations do not feel janky or overwhelming.
- Save/export/import settings are findable enough.

### First 5 minutes

- Player understands choosing an elemental dragon.
- Hatching feels rewarding.
- Tapping for Essence is obvious.
- First upgrades make sense.
- The dragon feels like the center of the experience.

### 5–15 minutes

- Auto adventure is understandable.
- Enemy defeats, loot, treasure, and equipment are noticed.
- Progression toward Drake feels visible.
- Daily goals/achievements are helpful rather than noisy.
- Rebirth being locked feels aspirational, not confusing.

## Expected baseline result

From release notes: Drake, Ember Woods, about 8-12 defeated enemies, 1 treasure, 0-1 equipment, EPS around 30-80, and Rebirth locked.

## Feedback prompts

Ask after the session:

1. What did you think you were supposed to do?
2. What felt satisfying?
3. What felt confusing or too small to read?
4. Did the dragon feel like a companion or just a button?
5. What would make you want to keep playing tomorrow?

## Do not change yet

- Early balance values.
- Core mechanics.
- Rebirth requirements.
- New monetization systems.

Only change those after repeated playtest evidence.
