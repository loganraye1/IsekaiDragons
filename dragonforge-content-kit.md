# Dragonforge Content Kit

## Visual Direction

Dragonforge should feel like a mythic creator guild powered by practical systems: volcanic forge, obsidian stone, molten light, brass/arcane machinery, subtle command-center tech, and dragons as the living identities behind the project.

Tone: hybrid fantasy + practical command center. Useful first, flavorful second.

---

## Channel Intro Posts

### #general

Welcome to **Dragonforge** — the common hall of the forge.

This is the place for everyday conversation, quick questions, ideas, updates, and anything that doesn’t need a dedicated command channel yet. Keep it useful, curious, and forge-friendly.

If something becomes a real task, move it toward **Mission Control**.

---

### #dragonforge-status

**Dragonforge Status** is the forge’s signal tower.

Use this channel for operational updates, availability notes, launches, outages, milestones, and anything the whole forge should know at a glance.

Keep posts clear and status-oriented:

- What changed
- Current state
- Any action needed
- Next check-in, if relevant

---

### #mission-control

Welcome to **Mission Control** — the command chamber of Dragonforge.

This is where active goals, decisions, coordination, and project momentum live. If the forge is building something, planning something, or tracking something, it belongs here.

Good Mission Control updates are:

- Clear
- Actionable
- Tied to a next step
- Easy to scan later

Bring the spark. Leave with a plan.
